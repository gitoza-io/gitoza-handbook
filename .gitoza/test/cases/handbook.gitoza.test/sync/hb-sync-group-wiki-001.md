# Wiki group lists changed Library pages

## Steps

1. On a **fork**, edit a Library (wiki) page and wait for autosave.
2. Click **Sync**.
3. Open the **Wiki** group in **Confirm Changes**.

## Expected Result

The edited wiki page appears under the **Wiki** group ([[GITO-SYNC02]] AC-4).
