# Ticket group lists changed tickets

## Steps

1. On a **fork**, edit a ticket (title, status, or body) and wait for autosave.
2. Click **Sync**.
3. Open the **Ticket** group in **Confirm Changes**.

## Expected Result

The edited ticket appears under the **Ticket** group ([[GITO-SYNC02]] AC-4).
